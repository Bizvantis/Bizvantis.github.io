03 / Research

Research papers

Two journal manuscripts on one question: can we trust a model's output on data it was never trained on?

Manuscript Target: IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics

Distribution-Free Battery State-of-Health Intervals for Unseen Cell Types: Hierarchical Cross-Domain Conformal Prediction With In-Service Check-Up Adaptation

Battery health models are trained on lab data, then deployed on cell types they have never seen. I showed that standard conformal prediction covers only 71.9% of cases at a 90% target there, and built HX-CP and HX-CP+ to fix it.

  • 90.1%coverage at a 90% target, with no labels from the new battery type
  • 30%lower interval score than the strongest baseline (Holm-adjusted p = 0.015)
  • 387Li-ion cells across 13 domains from 4 public datasets
Code on GitHub →
Manuscript Target: IEEE Transactions on Biometrics, Behavior, and Identity Science

FaceGuard: Continuous Face Authentication With a Homography-Parallax Liveness Cue for Commodity Webcams

A CPU-only system that adds face recognition, liveness checks and continuous presence authentication to any laptop webcam, evaluated on public benchmarks and a real-world study with phone-screen attacks.

  • 99.43%verification accuracy on Labeled Faces in the Wild
  • 0 / 59real phone-screen spoofing attacks accepted
  • 0.21%equal error rate on webcam images, plus a demographic fairness audit
